#  E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using **Power BI**.

The main purpose of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and understand important business areas such as **sales, profit, orders, quantity sold, discounts, products, categories, payment modes, and state-wise performance**.

The dataset contains around **15,000 sales records**. The project includes data cleaning, data analysis, business insights, and interactive dashboard creation.

---

##  Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Clean and prepare the sales dataset.
* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the best-performing states.
* Analyze sales by product category.
* Find the top 10 products by sales.
* Understand customer payment preferences.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Compare profit across product categories.
* Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales by State

State-wise sales were analyzed to identify which states generated the highest revenue.

**Key Insight:**
Rajasthan recorded the highest sales at approximately **₹7.23M**.

### 2. Sales by Product Category

Sales were analyzed across different product categories to understand category performance.

### 3. Top 10 Products by Sales

The top-performing products were identified based on their sales amount.

This helps understand which products contribute the most to overall revenue.

### 4. Payment Mode Analysis

Different payment modes were analyzed to understand customer payment preferences.

### 5. Profit by Product Category

Profit was compared across product categories to identify the categories contributing more to business profitability.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend

Monthly sales were analyzed to understand how sales changed over time and identify increases or decreases in business performance.


## Business Insights

Some important insights from the analysis include:

* The business generated approximately **₹49M in total sales**.
* Total profit was approximately **₹7.38M**.
* Around **15K orders** were recorded.
* Approximately **45K units** were sold.
* The average discount was around **15.01%**.
* **Rajasthan** generated the highest sales at approximately **₹7.23M**.
* Product, category, payment mode, and monthly trends were analyzed to understand business performance.
